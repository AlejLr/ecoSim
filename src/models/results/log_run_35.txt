# Environment Dynamics Documentation - Run #35

## 1. STEP ORDERING (Per Timestep)

Each timestep follows this exact sequence:

1. **Resource Spawning**
   - Grass grows on empty tiles at configured rate
   
2. **Agent Action Phase (Randomized Order)**
   - All agents execute their action in random order
   - Actions include: move, eat, idle, reproduce
   - Each agent receives immediate reward signal
   
3. **Energy Decay & Cleanup**
   - Apply per-step energy decay to all agents: -0.12 energy
   - Check for agent starvation (energy <= 0): mark as dead
   
4. **Dead Agent Removal**
   - Remove all marked-dead agents from environment
   - Prevent dead agents from affecting next step
   - Apply death penalty to their Q-table
   
5. **Reproduction Phase**
   - Check all living agents for reproduction eligibility
   - Execute pairwise mate-finding and breeding
   - Place offspring with appropriate starting energy

---

## 2. RESOURCE RULES

### Grass Spawning
- **Location**: Empty tiles (not water, not agent)
- **Energy Value**: 40 energy per grass
- **Consumption**: Prey eat grass, gain 40 energy
- **Regrowth**: Continuous regeneration at environment rate

### Energy System
- **Maximum**: 100 energy per agent
- **Decay**: -0.12 per step (base metabolic cost)
- **Movement Cost**: -0.15 per move action
- **Starting Energy - Prey**: 70
- **Starting Energy - Predator**: 85

---

## 3. KILL/REMOVAL RULES

### Starvation
- **Trigger**: Agent energy <= 0
- **Result**: Agent marked dead and removed
- **Penalty**: DEATH_PENALTY = -10
- **Timing**: Applied during cleanup phase after decay

### Predation
- **Trigger**: Predator executes EAT action on prey
- **Consequence**: 
  - Prey dies immediately (is_alive() = False)
  - Prey cannot act in future timesteps
  - Prevents double-kills (multiple predators eating same prey)
- **Energy Transfer**: Predator gains (prey_energy × 0.25)
  - Example: Prey with 50 energy → Predator gains 5 energy (10% efficiency)
- **Reward**: Predator gets energy_transferred + 15.0 bonus (hunting success reward)

---

## 4. REPRODUCTION RULES

### PREY Reproduction

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Energy >= 55
- reproduction_cooldown == 0 (not in cooldown from recent reproduction)
- Population < global carrying capacity (0.015 × grid area)
- Partner found: another prey with energy >= 55 and cooldown == 0

**Probability:**
- P(reproduce) = (energy_surplus / max_surplus) × 0.8
  - energy_surplus = energy - 55
  - max_surplus = 100 - 55
  - Example: Prey with 80 energy → (80-60)/(100-60) × 0.8 = 0.4 = 40% chance
  
**Energy Cost:**
- Parent 1 loses 25 energy
- Parent 2 loses 25 energy
- Offspring born with 50 energy

**Cooldown & Mate-Locking:**
- Both parents get reproduction_cooldown = 8
- Prevents same agents from reproducing twice in quick succession
- Mate-locking: Check mate.reproduction_cooldown > 0 to prevent double-mating

**Reward:**
- Both parents receive REPRODUCTION_REWARD = 15.0
- Encourages Q-learning policy to pursue reproduction

### PREDATOR Reproduction

**Dynamic Capacity:**
- Maximum predators allowed = prey_count × 0.5
- Prevents predator overpopulation
- If current_predators >= capacity, reproduction blocked

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Energy >= 70
- reproduction_cooldown == 0
- Below dynamic capacity
- Partner found: another predator with same criteria

**Probability:**
- P(reproduce) = (energy_surplus / max_surplus) × 0.7
  - energy_surplus = energy - 70
  - max_surplus = 100 - 70
  - Example: Predator with 90 energy → (90-80)/(100-80) × 0.7 ≈ 0.35 = 35% chance
  
**Energy Cost:**
- Parent 1 loses 35 energy
- Parent 2 loses 35 energy
- Offspring born with 45 energy

**Cooldown & Mate-Locking:**
- Both parents get reproduction_cooldown = 8
- Mate-locking: Check mate.reproduction_cooldown > 0

**Reward:**
- Both parents receive REPRODUCTION_REWARD = 15.0

## 5. REWARD SYSTEM

### Per-Action Rewards

| Action | Prey | Predator | Trigger |
|--------|------|----------|---------|
| Eating Grass | energy_gained × 1.0 | N/A | Grass consumed |
| Hunting | N/A | transfer × 1.0 + 0.25 | Prey killed |
| Reproduction | 0.5 | 0.5 | Offspring born |
| Step Penalty | -0.01 | -0.01 | Every step |
| Death Penalty | -10 | -10 | Agent dies |

## 6. OBSERVATION SPACE (Agent View)

Each agent observes 7 normalized dimensions [0, 1]:

1. **Energy**: current_energy / 100
2. **Target Distance** (Prey: predator, Predator: prey): normalized distance to nearest target
3. **Target Direction X** (Prey: predator, Predator: prey): -1 (left) to +1 (right)
4. **Target Direction Y** (Prey: predator, Predator: prey): -1 (up) to +1 (down)
5. **Target Detected** (Prey: predator, Predator: prey): 1 if nearby, 0 otherwise
6. **Can Reproduce**: 1 if eligible (energy >= threshold, cooldown=0, mate found), 0 otherwise
7. **Padding**: Placeholder dimension (unused, always 0 or 1)

State discretization: 2,160 unique states (5 × 3³ × 2³ combinations)

---

## 7. ACTION SPACE

Each agent can perform 10 actions:

| Index | Action | Effect |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 0-7 | Move (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW) | Move to adjacent tile, costs 0.15 energy |
| 8 | Eat | Consume grass (prey) or hunt (predator) |
| 9 | Idle | Do nothing |
